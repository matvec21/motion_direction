## Baselines

| ID | Method | Sources | Note |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| B1 | Essential matrix + RANSAC, no smoothing, no IMU | Nistér, Fischler & Bolles | The raw channel of our pipeline; already implemented. |
